Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone

Author: J.K. Rowling

Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone follows a young boy who discovers on his eleventh birthday that he is a wizard, and begins a new life at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry.

Why I love it: it is the book that made me fall in love with reading, and every time I return to it, it feels like coming home.
